# determinant
1. why need determinant ?
	1. example
		1. $Ax=0$, does $x$ has only solution $x=0$ ?
			1. has only $x=0 \rightarrow$  `nonsingular`
			2. has $x \ne 0 \rightarrow$ `singular`
		2. `nonsingular`$\Leftrightarrow$ $det(A) \ne 0$ 
2. recursive definition (<u>Laplace expansion</u>) ![[Pasted image 20260816121803.png]]
3. properties
	1. row operations #why
		1. row op. 1 $\rightarrow$ $(-1) \cdot det(A)$
		2. row op. 2 $\rightarrow$ $k \cdot det(A)$
		3. row op. 3 $\rightarrow$ $det(A)$ ===不影響 det(A) 的值===
	2. $det(AB) = det(A)det(B)$
		1. when $A=B$, $det(A^2)=(det(A))^2$
	3. $det(A^k) = det(A)^k$
	4. $det(A^T) = det(A)$
	5. column operations (`same as row operations`)
		1. col op. 1 $\rightarrow$ $(-1) \cdot det(A)$
		2. col op. 2 $\rightarrow$ $k \cdot det(A)$
		3. col op. 3 $\rightarrow$ $det(A)$ ===不影響 det(A) 的值===
	6. diagonal matrix D
		1. $$
			D =
			\begin{bmatrix}
			d_1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\
			0 & d_2 & \cdots & 0 \\
			\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
			0 & 0 & \cdots & d_n
			\end{bmatrix}
			$$
		2. $det(D) = d_1 \cdots d_n$
